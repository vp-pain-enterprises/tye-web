Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

Short version: Tye stores everything locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data or the information you store about other people. Your vault is truly yours.

1. Who we are

Tye is an iOS application developed and operated by Pain Enterprises ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at tye@pain-enterprises.org.

2. What data Tye stores

Tye is a local-first application. All data you create inside Tye — including people you add, memories you log, vault entries, milestones, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's AsyncStorage mechanism.

We have no access to this data. It does not leave your device unless you explicitly back it up through iOS iCloud backup (which is managed entirely by Apple, not by us).

Data stored locally includes:

  • Names, relationship types, and photos of people you add
  • Vault items (preferences, places, gift ideas) per person
  • Memory logs and milestone dates
  • App settings (appearance, notification preferences)
  • Purchase status (to avoid showing the paywall after purchase)

Data we do NOT collect:

  • No account or registration is required
  • No personal data is transmitted to our servers
  • No location data is collected
  • No contacts from your address book are accessed without explicit action
  • No advertising identifiers are used

3. Analytics

Tye uses PostHog for anonymised product analytics. This helps us understand how features are used in aggregate — such as which screens are most visited — so we can improve the app.

Analytics are opt-in only. You will be asked for consent during onboarding and can change your preference at any time in Settings. If you opt out, no analytics data is collected or transmitted.

The following is collected only if you consent:

  • Anonymised screen views and feature interactions
  • Crash reports and performance metrics
  • Device type and iOS version (non-identifying)

No personally identifiable information (PII) — including names, emails, or any data you enter into the app — is ever included in analytics events. PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

4. In-app purchases

Tye offers a one-time lifetime purchase processed through Apple's App Store. We do not handle payment details directly — all billing is managed by Apple.

Purchase status is cached locally on your device via RevenueCat, a third-party service used to verify and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous user identifier for this purpose. Their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

Tye can send local push notifications to remind you of milestones and relationship check-ins. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device — they do not involve any remote server or network request.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app's Settings or in your iOS device settings. You can also enable "private mode," which hides the names of people from notification previews on your lock screen.

6. Third-party services

Tye integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store — app distribution and payment processing
  • RevenueCat — purchase verification and restoration
  • PostHog — anonymised analytics (opt-in only)

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns about data handling by these providers.

7. Children's privacy

Tye is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store as it contains no objectionable content, but it is intended for adult use.

8. Data security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms — passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and iOS encryption. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

We have deliberately chosen not to implement server-side storage in order to minimise the attack surface for your personal data.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, most data subject rights (access, rectification, deletion) are exercised directly on your device:

  • Access / Export: Your data exists in your device's local storage
  • Deletion: Delete individual entries within the app, or uninstall Tye to remove all data
  • Opt-out of analytics: Toggle in Settings → Privacy
  • Notification opt-out: Toggle in Settings → Notifications or iOS Settings

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not sell personal data, the right to opt out of sale does not apply. For any CCPA-related enquiries, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted within the app or on this page. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
